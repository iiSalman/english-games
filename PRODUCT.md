# PRODUCT

**register: product**

## Product purpose
A small web game that teaches English vocabulary to a young Arabic-speaking child,
built around the *We Can! 2* (Grade 2, term 2) workbook. Each theme is a guided
journey: Learn (connect word↔picture) → Listen test → Spell test, with a star reward
that persists across sessions.

## Users
- **Primary:** one child, ~8 years old, native Arabic speaker, beginner in English,
  an early/uncertain reader in both languages. Plays on a phone or tablet, often alone,
  sometimes with a parent nearby. Short attention span; motivated by reward and play.
- **Secondary:** a parent who sets the child up and may want simple controls
  (sound, progress) and protection from destructive actions.

## Tone & brand
Warm, playful, encouraging. Bright but not chaotic. Big friendly shapes, generous
touch targets, audio everywhere, reward-only feedback (never punish a wrong answer).
Arabic interface (RTL); the English target words are the only English on screen.

## Anti-references
- Not a clinical ed-tech dashboard (no dense text, no charts, no muted "professional" palette).
- Not babyish-cluttered (no busy confetti everywhere, no cartoon mascots crowding the screen).
- Not a quiz app that shames wrong answers with red X's and scores that go down.

## Strategic principles
1. **Playable without reading.** Pictures + audio carry meaning; Arabic text is support, never a gate.
2. **Huge, obvious touch targets.** A small hand on a phone must hit things easily.
3. **Forgiving.** Wrong answers cost nothing; the child retries until they succeed.
4. **Reward, don't rank.** Stars only go up. Every correct answer celebrates.
5. **Protect the child.** Destructive or external actions must be hard to trigger by accident.
